# Herald Agent — Long-Term Vision

**Everything, handled automatically.**

Herald is evolving from a high-autonomy CRM and side-hustle operations engine into a **goal-driven personal autonomous agent**—an always-on digital chief of staff that plans, executes, observes, and adapts across your businesses and daily life with minimal friction.

---

## 1. Goal-Driven Autonomous Agent Core

Instead of waiting for manual triggers, Herald operates on a continuous **Plan -> Act -> Observe** loop:

- **Proactive Heartbeat & Event Wakeups**: Scheduled heartbeats and real-time event listeners (inbound messages, pipeline state changes, webhook signals) wake the agent when action is needed.
- **Persistent Episodic Memory**: Long-term memory of past runs, client interactions, preferences, and outcomes so every decision compounds over time.
- **1-Tap Human-in-the-Loop Approval**: High-stakes actions (outbound sends, payments, deployments) pause for instant 1-tap approval via Telegram or the Companion Mobile App, keeping you in control without micromanagement.

## 2. Multi-Side-Hustle Pluggable Niche Packs

Herald's core engine—discovery, demo generation, outreach, and pipeline tracking—is designed to be **niche-agnostic**:

- **Pluggable Niche Packs**: Drop in a new vertical pack (e.g., dental clinics, real estate agencies, SaaS founders, local fitness studios) with custom discovery queries, demo templates, scoring rubrics, and outreach sequences.
- **Isolated Hustle Workspaces**: Run multiple side hustles concurrently with separate leads, KPIs, and budgets from one unified operations engine.

## 3. Social Media & Video Content Automation

Beyond 1-to-1 outreach, Herald will automate **1-to-many distribution**:

- **Automated Content Pipelines**: Turn product updates, case studies, and live demos into short-form video scripts, screen recordings, and social posts.
- **Multi-Platform Scheduling & Engagement**: Publish across X, LinkedIn, Instagram, and YouTube Shorts, monitor replies, and route warm inbound interest directly into the CRM pipeline.

## 4. Full Personal Assistant

Herald will unify business operations with **personal productivity**:

- **Day Planning & Prioritization**: Morning briefings that synthesize calendar events, urgent CRM follow-ups, and deep-work blocks.
- **Meeting Scheduling & Prep**: Automated scheduling links, pre-meeting lead/context dossiers, and post-meeting action-item extraction.
- **Unified Messaging**: Triage and draft replies across email, WhatsApp, Instagram, and Telegram from a single command center.

## 5. Companion Mobile App

Take Herald everywhere with a dedicated **iOS & Android companion app**:

- **Daily Briefings**: Glanceable summaries of overnight agent runs, pipeline movement, and revenue metrics.
- **1-Tap Approvals**: Approve or tweak queued outreach, demo deploys, and agent plans on the go.
- **Voice & Chat Command**: Talk to Herald naturally to check status, kick off a discovery batch, or log a quick note.
